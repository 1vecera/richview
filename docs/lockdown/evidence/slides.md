# Number-first slides: what the evidence says

Web research of 17 Nov 2026 for the slides card (FEA-RP-65). Every claim here was read on the linked page.

## How people build these screens today

- **Slide tools use a fixed 16:9 canvas scaled to the window.** reveal.js lays out at 960 × 700 and scales between `minScale` 0.2 and `maxScale` 2.0 ([presentation size](https://revealjs.com/presentation-size/)); Marp wraps each slide in a 1280 × 720 SVG `viewBox` and needs no script ([inline SVG](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/marp-team/marpit/main/docs/inline-svg.md)); Slidev has `canvasWidth` 980 and `aspectRatio` 16/9 ([customizations](https://sli.dev/custom/)); Gamma lays slides out "on a fixed-size 16:9 canvas" ([Gamma help](https://help.gamma.app/en/articles/17572896-how-do-grids-and-freeform-editing-work-in-gamma)); Power BI pages default to 16:9 with Fit to page ([display settings](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/create-reports/power-bi-report-display-settings)).
- **Dashboard tools reflow.** Observable Framework's `grid grid-cols-4` drops to 2 and 1 columns by container width, with a `.big` number class at `700 32px` inside a `.card` ([Markdown](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/observablehq/framework/main/docs/markdown.md), [grid.css](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/observablehq/framework/main/src/style/grid.css)). Tableau warns that Automatic sizing "can lead to unpredictable results on different screens" and says to author at the final display size ([sizing](https://help.tableau.com/current/pro/desktop/en-us/dashboards_organize_floatingandtiled.htm), [best practices](https://help.tableau.com/current/pro/desktop/en-us/dashboards_best_practices.htm)).
- **A slide starts at a heading or a `---`.** Quarto: `##` makes a slide, `#` a section slide, `---` also separates ([Quarto revealjs](https://quarto.org/docs/presentations/revealjs/)). Marp, Slidev and Gamma split at `---` ([Marpit directives](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/marp-team/marpit/main/docs/directives.md), [Slidev syntax](https://sli.dev/guide/syntax), [Gamma cards](https://help.gamma.app/en/articles/11016396-what-are-cards-in-gamma-and-how-to-do-they-work)).
- **The closest declared big number is Evidence's `<BigValue>`:** `value`, `title`, `comparison`, `comparisonTitle`, `downIsGood`, `sparkline`, and "multiple cards will align themselves into a row" ([BigValue v2.2.1](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/evidence-dev/evidence/v2.2.1/sites/docs/docs/components/big-value.md)). Power BI's KPI visual takes a base measure, a target and a "High is good" setting ([KPI visual](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/power-bi/visuals/power-bi-visualization-kpi)). Slidev has a `fact` layout for one prominent figure ([layouts](https://sli.dev/builtin/layouts)). Keynote, Google Slides and Pitch are drag-and-drop: nothing declarative to copy.

## Fit and overflow

- **No tool handles overflow well.** Marp: content "may still overflow the bottom of a slide" ([Marp Core](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/marp-team/marp-core/main/docs/markdown.md)). Gamma shrinks to a minimum size, then shows a grey stripe and tells the author to split the slide ([Gamma help](https://help.gamma.app/en/articles/17572896-how-do-grids-and-freeform-editing-work-in-gamma)). Quarto offers `.smaller` or `.scrollable` by hand ([Quarto revealjs](https://quarto.org/docs/presentations/revealjs/)). reveal.js fits one text element with `r-fit-text` and stretches one child with `r-stretch` ([layout](https://revealjs.com/layout/)).
- **On a phone, slide tools switch to scrolling.** reveal.js turns its scroll view on at mobile widths, and Quarto makes it the "default view on small screens" ([scroll view](https://revealjs.com/scroll-view/), [Quarto presenting](https://quarto.org/docs/presentations/revealjs/presenting.html)). A 16:9 canvas letterboxed on an upright phone would use about a third of the screen's height (our arithmetic).

## What makes a number read at a glance

- **Context is one comparison.** Stephen Few: a number needs "one or more comparisons", so "choose the one best comparison … two comparisons at most", and trend when it is useful; a dashboard is one screen read at a glance, without scrolling ([Rich Data, Poor Data](https://www.perceptualedge.com/articles/Whitepapers/Rich_Data_Poor_Data.pdf)).
- **Never colour alone.** WCAG 1.4.1 ([Use of Color](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/use-of-color.html)); Plotly's card guide adds a sign and a named reference such as "vs last week", and reverses the colour where a fall is good ([KPI card guide](https://plotly.com/blog/kpi-card-guide/)). NN/g: colour works as a second cue, not as the carrier of magnitude ([preattentive attributes](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/dashboards-preattentive/)).
- **Type at a distance.** AVIXA's display standard sets a lowercase letter at 2 to 4% of the screen's height, 3% as a start ([DISCAS](https://www.avixa.org/standards/discas-calculators/discas/learn-more-about-display-size)). Apple's television sizes are 29 pt by default and 23 pt at least on a 1080-line screen, 2.7% and 2.1% of its height as font size ([HIG accessibility](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility.md)). Anthropic's PowerPoint skill sets body text at 14 pt on a 7.5 in slide, 2.6% ([pptx skill](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anthropics/skills/main/skills/pptx/SKILL.md)).

## Density

- Published numbers are heuristics: "two or three" views per dashboard (Tableau, above); 3 to 4 big numbers, never ten at equal weight ([practitioner newsletter](https://vizmasters.substack.com/p/big-ass-numbers-bans-why-they-belong)); past about eight cards a table compares better (Plotly, above); Few's "about eight chunks" of working memory.
- We found no study behind 6×6, 7×7 or 10/20/30; Duarte's three-second glance test is a craft rule ([Glance Test](https://www.duarte.com/resources/guides-tools/the-glance-test/)).

## What an agent should write

- **Overflow is the failure.** Anthropic's pptx skill calls text overflow "the most common defect and always user-visible", and python-pptx saves off-canvas text without a warning ([pptx skill](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anthropics/skills/main/skills/pptx/SKILL.md), [SlideForge](https://slideforge.dev/blog/python-pptx-limitations-we-solved)). Models writing slide code without seeing it make "frequent errors such as text overflow, misalignment, and inconsistent spacing" ([arXiv 2502.15412](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.15412v1)).
- **What prevents it:** no coordinates, layouts chosen for the author, a canvas whose fit is computable, a type floor, counts capped per slide, and diagnostics that name the slide. We found no tool with all of them.

## Keys, links and paper

- reveal.js and Marp share arrows, Space, Page Up and Down, Home, End and `F` for full screen ([reveal keyboard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hakimel/reveal.js/master/js/controllers/keyboard.js), [Marp navigation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/marp-team/marp-cli/main/src/templates/bespoke/navigation.ts)). Marp's address is `#3`, counted from 1; reveal's is `#/2`, from 0 ([Marp state](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/marp-team/marp-cli/main/src/templates/bespoke/state.ts), [reveal links](https://revealjs.com/links/)).
- One slide per page is `@page { size: <width> <height>; margin: 0 }`, which reveal.js writes at print time and every current browser supports since December 2024 ([print view](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hakimel/reveal.js/master/js/controllers/printview.js), [MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/@page/size)).

## What the design takes from it

- A 16:9 stage whose sizes are shares of the stage, so one composition holds at every landscape size; a scrolling stack on a phone.
- Slides start at a heading or `---`; the author writes content and RichView places it, with no coordinates.
- The existing `metric` block is the big number: one comparison, named in words, sign printed, colour second.
- Text at 2.7% of the stage's height and nothing under 2.1%, the labels under the numbers and a chart's lettering included; no shrinking to fit; counts checked per slide and reported by slide and line; the fix is always a new slide.
- A short table is set larger the fewer and shorter its rows, up to 4.3% of the stage's height, so it reads across a room as the numbers do.
- The numbers a slide stands on are its metrics. A chart on a slide prints none of the values it would add by itself, so no bar carries a number its neighbour lacks, and it shows none of its actions; its author can ask for the values back. This came from the review of the first sheet, not from the sources above.
- reveal.js's and Marp's keys, Marp's address counted from 1 (`#slide=4`), and one slide per page on PowerPoint's 13.333 × 7.5 in page; `richview generate --to pdf` lays the slides out at that page before printing.
- A slide that still overflows grows and scrolls, which no surveyed tool does; a measured overflow check is left for a later card.

## The look

Daniel judged the first sheet "basic" and asked for "the most kicks ass slides ever made - pure aesthetic joy for all of them" (17 Nov 2026). A second pass the same evening read how keynote stages, editorial graphics and studio work stage a number, a chart and a pause; every link was opened that day, and Apple's slides were sampled from its public event streams.

- **Scale does the work.** One thing is huge and the rest footnote-sized. Apple's 9 Sept 2025 stream sets "120Hz" in one colour on black, its unit as large as its number and no label beside it ([Apple events](https://www.apple.com/apple-events/)). Stripe Sessions sets its display type at weight 250, line height 85% and tracking -0.04em, so light letters at a huge size read as one shape ([stripesessions.com](https://stripesessions.com/)); Vercel's Ship pages track Geist headings at -0.04 to -0.06em ([vercel.com/ship](https://vercel.com/ship)).
- **Colour is a field, and changing the field is the rhythm.** Apple cuts between black and a pale grey ground, the product's own colour its one accent; Stripe uses one deep plum field; Spotify Wrapped makes the typeface itself the picture ([It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/features/spotify-wrapped-2024-graphic-design-041224)).
- **Quiet slides earn the loud ones.** Apple's quiet slides are about 70% empty: three short centred lines on pale grey. Businessweek's designer calls display type and graphics "syncopated moments" over plain bricks and mortar, and charts there "start small and scale up according to their merit" ([Eye](https://eyemagazine.com/feature/article/taking-care-of-business)).
- **A chart is staged by removal and a sentence.** The Economist's guide gives a chart a short red tag over a title that is only 19% larger than its subtitle, light gridlines and direct labels ([style guide, 2017](https://sa.ipaa.org.au/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Economist-CHARTstyleguide_20170505.pdf)); FT and Bloomberg graphics editors ask titles to make the point, with a verb ([Burn-Murdoch in Eye](https://www.eyemagazine.com/feature/article/conversation-with-john-burn-murdoch), [Amanda Cox](https://www.datawrapper.de/blog/amanda-cox-bloomberg)).
- **Posters set type as the image.** Paula Scher's Public Theater work sets wood-type and condensed capitals on a hard grid with heavy rules ([Pentagram](https://www.pentagram.com/work/the-public-theater)).
- **A drawn talk embraces imperfection.** Dear Data's postcards and Giorgia Lupi's manifesto keep a defined range of colours and let the hand show ([Dear Data](https://www.dear-data.com/theproject), [manifesto](http://giorgialupi.com/data-humanism-my-manifesto-for-a-new-data-world)); rough-notation draws an underline or a circle in about 800 ms as a gesture ([rough-notation](https://github.com/rough-stuff/rough-notation)).
- **A paper's figure is plain and numbered.** Tufte CSS keeps an off-white page, a serif for statements and a sans for labels ([Tufte CSS](https://edwardtufte.github.io/tufte-css/)); Distill sets section heads over a 1 px rule and captions at 60% black ([Distill template](https://distill.pub/template.v2.js)); Nature labels panels with bold lowercase letters ([final artwork guide](https://www.nature.com/documents/nature-final-artwork.pdf)).
- **Motion is short and single.** Apple's events page fades in 500 ms; Stripe's list items enter in 0.3 s; a drawn stroke takes about 0.8 s.

What the stages take from it: each style's own world rather than one layout recoloured; fields for the loud slides (Sleek's accent for the lone number and the section, Brutalist's accent and ink); Air's light figures and its one quiet raised field; condensed capitals to the slide's edges and an accent tag over each working title (Scher, the Economist's tag); lettering at a tilt with the red pencil drawn on arrival; a title page, rules and numbered figures for Scientific; arrivals at each style's own pace. Not taken here: charts cropped past the slide's edges without axes, hand-drawn chart marks and lettered bar values, and panel letters on paired charts, which need the chart engine and wait for their own cards.
