Scope: every style, theme, design token, font, palette, motion rule and design principle of RichView on `origin/main` at `b4db233` (merge of PR #61, 2026-10-16), read from the lockdown worktree on 2026-10-21. Also read: the open branches `task/s27-654-chart-polish` (PR #67, head `47d8848`, 2026-10-19) and `task/s27-653-site` (PR #69, head `5e69843`, 2026-10-19); merged PRs #58 and #59 (2026-10-13); the private `richview-universe/richview-design-system` repository at `8c6a8e0` (2026-06-21), read-only through `gh` and cited as `design-system:<path>`; Linear S27-404, S27-407, S27-578, S27-616, S27-653 and S27-654; Daniel's legacy local notes on Claw Mini "Daniel's manual design system" (2026-06-21), "Design system — anti-slop house style" (2026-06-09), "Suggested tasks — from the 2026-06-20 design-system session" and the research digest "Exa deep-research runs — RichView design system (2026-06-21)", cited as "Daniel's notes: <title> (<date>)" as in `evidence/intent-notion-site.md`; the Notion page "v1 — the original RichView startup (therichview.com, 2023)", cited by title because it is private.

Method: read the five builtin `.rstyle.yaml` files, `report.css`, `print.css`, the style resolver, the chart colour, palette, SVG and motion modules, the showcase, homepage and landing hosts, and every design document and rule; recomputed contrast and OKLab separation with an independent script; rendered a fictional probe report (`docs/lockdown/screenshots/styles-design/probe-report.rmd`) with each builtin and with an explicit `--theme`, then read computed styles in headless Chromium at 1440×900 and 390×844 after `window.__richview.settled()`; inspected the flagship showcase through a loopback `richview-server` serving `dist/web`. Nothing was deployed, published, posted or committed.

Confidence: **observed** = verified in code at `b4db233` or in a probe render (line or probe cited); **stated** = written in a doc, PR, ticket or note and not verified; **inferred** = my reading across sources; **unknown** = no evidence found. Paths are repo-relative. In tables, colours are `oklch(L C H)` written as `L C H`.

## Key findings

- **Five treatments, six names, three defaults.** The core ships `sleek` (dark; `sleek-dark` loads the same file), `sleek-light`, `scientific`, `brutalist` and `hand-drawn`, plus `editorial` as a deprecated alias of `brutalist` (`crates/richview-core/src/style.rs:16-45,204-211`). With no style the core renders `brutalist` (`crates/richview-core/src/render.rs:103-105`), the flagship showcase opens in `scientific` (`crates/richview-web/src/browser/showcase.rs:157`) and the homepage demo in `sleek` (`assets/landing/demo/report.rmd:7`), while S27-616 (2026-10-10) states "Sleek dark — default". Observed and stated.
- **The identity was decided in June and has drifted since.** The TS-era house style was "The Economist meets Linear" (warm sand hue 40, one indigo accent, light and dark through `prefers-color-scheme`; Daniel's notes: Design system — anti-slop house style (2026-06-09), lines 8-17), and the 2026-06-20 studio proposed an `editorial` house style in the same colours with Newsreader and IBM Plex Mono. On 2026-06-21 Daniel chose three templates instead, each with its own fonts and a blue accent ("Claude is orange and we're blue"): brutalist as the default, sleek (dark, after the Lamplight variant) and scientific (working paper), with each setting limited to three options (Daniel's notes: Daniel's manual design system (2026-06-21), lines 4-35). The same day's research digest turned the brief into font stacks and accents (`#124A8C`, `#195BFF`, `#1B6F9E`), and the Rust builtins carry exactly those picks (Daniel's notes: Exa deep-research runs — RichView design system (2026-06-21), lines 12-20; OKLab ΔE 0 after conversion), but S27-616 (2026-10-10) made sleek dark the default and added sleek-light and a red-accented hand-drawn, the style grammar exposes every token rather than three-option settings, and S27-404 and S27-407 remain open in Brain Dump. The TS-era sand and indigo survive as fallbacks in `report.css`, the chrome tokens, the pencil marks and the showcase base. Stated, observed and inferred.
- **An explicit `theme: light|dark` breaks every builtin.** The `html[data-theme]` blocks in `crates/richview-core/assets/report.css:45-62` outrank the style's `:root` tokens and replace seven tokens with the TS-era sand and indigo values, while `chart-ink`, `bg-chart` and the semantic colours keep the style's values. Chart axis text falls to 1.11:1 (scientific + dark) and 1.05:1 (sleek + light); `theme: auto` never switches to dark; `richview check` reports the broken render `clean`. Observed (screenshots `defect-theme-*`). S27-593 reported the sleek + light case on 2026-10-05 (per `evidence/linear.md`).
- **Builtin chart palettes meet the stated colour rules.** Recomputed: slots 1–4 reach 3.19–5.30:1 on their chart surfaces, neighbouring slots differ by OKLab ΔE×100 ≥ 15.4, and by ≥ 8.5 under simulated protan, deutan and tritan vision. The rule is called "director decision D25" (with D26) only in code comments (`richview-chart/src/color.rs:9`, `crates/richview-core/tests/palettes.rs:1`); no definition exists in docs, Linear or commit messages. Observed; definition unknown.
- **The showcase is a second, older design system.** Its 23 treatments (3 production, 20 studio variants) come from a TS-era stylesheet snapshot (`assets/showcase/themes.css:1`, "snapshot c617606") that fills stat cards, puts side bars on callouts, sets stats in mono at 2.5rem and uses original font names. It has no `sleek-light` or `hand-drawn`, and its `scientific` and `brutalist` palettes fail the D25 separation rule (ΔE×100 2.6 and 7.1). This contradicts the core's "none fills a panel" and "callouts have no side bar". Observed.
- **Motion policy is coherent on paper; parts are unbuilt.** `docs/MOTION.md` and `richview-chart/src/motion.rs` define two curves, data-only motion, a reduced-motion hard floor and settled exports; the status section lists missing stroke growth, bar brightening, shadows and drawn-on pencil strokes. Report text never animates in the core; the showcase still declares text fade-ups without keyframes; the landing's scroll stage was removed in PR #58 and is being restored in PR #69. Observed and stated.
- **Typography is per style and licensed, with two rule breaks.** Each builtin sets one type scale and one measure, but the stated "adjacent sizes at least 1.2x apart" fails at ≤640 px, where `size-h1` equals `size-h2` in every builtin. Core faces are pinned OFL builds renamed to avoid reserved names (`RichView Serif/Sans/Code/Mono`), while the landing and reel declare subset files as "Source Serif 4" and "IBM Plex Mono" and the docs call the same Plex file "Docs Mono". Observed; licence risk inferred.
- **Static chart frames do not match default figure widths.** Frames are drawn at 358 and 832 px, but default figures sit inside the 512–576 px measure, so before the runtime mounts (and without JavaScript) chart text renders at 67–69% in sleek and scientific and at 143–152% in brutalist and hand-drawn. After mount every chart repaints at its real width. Observed.
- **Guidance is partly stale.** `.claude/rules/anti-slop-design.md` points to `assets/styles/` tokens (chrome-only) and to landing stages that are dormant on main; `docs/public/styles.md:69` and `crates/richview-core/src/knowledge.rs:15` say hand-drawn chart text is monospaced while the style and RMD-SPEC set Patrick Hand; `llms.txt:13`, `CLAUDE.md:76` and the skill name three styles; the primary checkout's `CLAUDE.md` (local `main` at `5ceb7d4`) still states "The Economist meets Linear". Observed.
- **The open PRs overlap.** PR #67 and PR #69 both lighten the tooltip (surface 82% → 70%, border 78% → 60%) and label the chart actions. PR #67 also adds `layout.measure-wide-blocks`, five primary style pills that `themes.json` cannot fill, a white PNG backdrop and a fixed menu shadow. Observed (diff); effects inferred.

## Builtin styles (S-SLEEK-DARK, S-SLEEK-LIGHT, S-SCIENTIFIC, S-BRUTALIST, S-HAND-DRAWN)

Each builtin is one declarative YAML file compiled into the core and validated against the same grammar as user styles at first use; an invalid builtin panics (`crates/richview-core/src/style.rs:27-36,179-199`) (observed). Sleek, scientific and brutalist came with the Rust port `2c926b0` (2026-10-04), sleek-light with `41aac28` (2026-10-05, PR #16), hand-drawn with `f388b5f` (2026-10-11) (observed, `git log`).

Intent stated in each file header: sleek is "Dark, cool-hued (260°) Material-grey surfaces + a single poppy-ultramarine accent" (`crates/richview-core/assets/sleek.rstyle.yaml:1-3`); sleek-light has "white surfaces, black chart ink and the vivid Sleek series palette" (`sleek-light.rstyle.yaml:1-2`); scientific is a "Light warm-paper (70°) academic working-paper ... Steel-blue accent, calm and credible" (`scientific.rstyle.yaml:1-3`); brutalist is "Light warm paper, maximally high-contrast: rule == ink (black rules), 4px header bars, uppercase condensed display ... the studio's BASE template" (`brutalist.rstyle.yaml:1-4`); hand-drawn is "Ink on off-white paper ... one red-pencil accent kept for the single most important mark, the stat underline", with paper, ink and every grey on one warm hue (`hand-drawn.rstyle.yaml:1-10`) (stated).

Lineage (observed): the three original builtins are the 2026-06-21 research digest's picks. Its verdict gives brutalist Big Shoulders + Newsreader + Space Mono with `#124A8C`, sleek Space Grotesk + Geist + Geist Mono with `#195BFF`, and scientific Source Serif 4 + Source Sans 3 + Source Code Pro with `#1B6F9E`, each tuned from Daniel's anchors `#144E95`, `#0433FF` and `#1A6DAC` "for AAA contrast and template character" (Daniel's notes: Exa deep-research runs — RichView design system (2026-06-21), lines 12-20, stated). Converted to OKLCH, those hexes equal the builtin accents exactly (`0.4128 0.1233 255.65`, `0.5464 0.2508 263.51`, `0.5165 0.1062 239.23`), and the fonts are the shipped stacks with the Source families renamed. The digest also grounds sleek's dark grey surfaces ("wow through dark-surface depth, not pure-black/pure-white glare", digest line 90) and warns that Geist alone "will read as polished but too Vercel/SaaS-generic" (digest line 78). Sleek-light reuses the sleek accent; hand-drawn's red pencil `0.52 0.174 31` is the only accent that is not blue. The brief's sources match the showcase: the only dark studio variant is v04 "Original, Lamplight", and v13 is "Working Paper" (`assets/showcase/themes.json`).

## Cascade, grammar and safety (S-CASCADE, S-TOKENS, S-PHONE-TOKENS, S-STYLE-SETTINGS, S-STYLE-GRAMMAR, S-RETIRED-LEAVES)

- References: each frontmatter `styles` entry is a builtin name, `editorial`, or the name of a style file the host resolved into `options.data`; anything else is the hard error `style.unresolved` (`crates/richview-core/src/render.rs:82-101`). CLI `--style` references follow the source styles (`render.rs:102`; `docs/public/styles.md:40`). An unknown name reaching `resolve_styles` directly gives the warning `style.unknown` (`style.rs:229-231`). Observed.
- Merge: sections `tokens`, `layout`, `chart`, `motion`, `components`; later styles replace earlier values per top-level key of each section, so arrays and whole component objects (for example `components.big`) are replaced, not merged (`style.rs:451-467`; `docs/RMD-SPEC.md:339`). Observed.
- Output: `tokens` and `layout` become `--rv-*` custom properties on `:root`; every `-phone` size token replaces its base token inside `@media (max-width: 640px)` (`style.rs:126-149,256-264`). CSS order in a report: font faces, `report.css`, style CSS (tokens, italic and bold rules, components, reduced-motion floor), `EXTRA_CSS`, chart CSS, figure CSS, frame CSS, `print.css`, print tokens (`render.rs:308-318`). Observed.
- Vocabulary: `tokens` = 17 colours, 4 font stacks, 6 sizes (each with `-phone`), `line-base`, `measure`, `radius-s`, `radius-m`; `layout` = `report-max-width`, `margin-column`; `motion` = `enabled`; `components` = `big`, `deck`, `callout`, `header`, `rules`, `title`, `heading`, `table-head`, `source-label` (`style.rs:541-629`; `docs/RMD-SPEC.md:343-347`). Observed.
- Customization surface (S-STYLE-SETTINGS): the 2026-06-21 brief limited the style file to three options per setting (density, block gap, font size), a fixed line spacing, font families with full override and one of three blue accents, with "no way to adj. it in any heavier way" in v1 (Daniel's notes: Daniel's manual design system (2026-06-21), lines 22-35) (stated). The shipped grammar has no density, block-gap or font-size presets; it exposes every token above with free values inside the safety grammar, and the report's block gap is one body line (`--rv-line`, 27–30 px in the builtins) rather than 4 rem (`report.css:71-79`) (observed).
- Safety grammar: colours are `oklch|lch|lab|oklab|hsl(a)|rgb(a)(...)`, 3–8 digit hex or a bare keyword, ≤80 characters; fonts `[a-zA-Z0-9_ .,'"+-]`, ≤200; dimensions a number with `px|ch|rem|em|%|vh|vw|fr|s|ms`, ≤20; weights 1–1000 or keywords (`style.rs:472-514`). Observed.
- The `chart` section is supplier-owned: only `ink`, `ink-soft`/`inkSoft`, `grid`, `accent`, `background`, `muted`, `font-family`/`fontFamily`, `colors`/`seriesColors` and `line.smoothing` are checked; every other key, including `palette`, `ramp` and `fontText`, passes (`style.rs:630-650`). ## Typography and fonts (S-TYPE-SCALE, S-FONT-CATALOG, S-FONT-NAMING, S-FONT-SYNTHESIS, S-FONT-LOADING)

## Colour scheme and print (S-COLOR-SCHEME, S-PRINT)

- `theme: auto|light|dark` in frontmatter, or `--theme`, is documented as "Color-scheme hint; style tokens retain their own color choices. Default `auto`" (`docs/RMD-SPEC.md:44`; `docs/public/styles.md:7`) (stated). The mechanism dates from the single house style, whose note promised light and dark "auto via `prefers-color-scheme`, plus explicit `theme:` override" (Daniel's notes: Design system — anti-slop house style (2026-06-09), line 17) (stated). The renderer writes `data-theme` only for `light` and `dark` (`render.rs:295-307`). `report.css` declares the TS-era dark tokens twice, under `prefers-color-scheme: dark` on `:root` and on `html[data-theme="dark"]`, plus light tokens on `html[data-theme="light"]`; its comment says the dark set is "applied via `prefers-color-scheme (auto) AND html[data-theme="dark"] (explicit override)`" (`report.css:31-62`). With `auto`, the style's later `:root` block wins, so no builtin ever turns dark, and sleek stays dark under a light preference (observed: all builtins rendered identically under light and dark `colorScheme`). ## Reproduction

Build the worktree (`cargo run --release --locked -p richview-build`), copy `docs/lockdown/screenshots/styles-design/probe-report.rmd` to an empty folder as `report.rmd`, and render it once per style; outputs land in that folder's `Rendered/`:

```sh
target/release/richview generate <folder>/report.rmd --style sleek --public-dir "$PWD/dist/web" -o sleek.html --format json
target/release/richview generate <folder>/report.rmd --style scientific --theme dark --public-dir "$PWD/dist/web" -o scientific-themedark.html --format json
target/release/richview generate <folder>/report.rmd --style sleek --theme light --public-dir "$PWD/dist/web" -o sleek-themelight.html --format json
target/release/richview-server --public-dir "$PWD/dist/web" --data-dir <scratch> --port 18731   # showcase at /showcase/openai-vs-anthropic/?theme=sleek
```

Open each file in headless Chromium (Playwright, `reducedMotion: "reduce"`), wait until `window.__richview.settled` exists, await `settled()`, then read `getComputedStyle` of `body`, `.rv-report__title`, `.rv-big`, `.rv-callout`, `.rv-report__body h2` and the visible `svg.rvc-svg text`; for static frames, measure `svg.rvc-svg` width against its `viewBox` before mount. Contrast and separation: convert OKLCH → OKLab → linear sRGB (Ottosson), take WCAG 2 relative luminance, Euclidean OKLab distance ×100, and colour-vision simulation with the Machado et al. 2009 severity-1 matrices in linear sRGB, as `richview-chart/src/color.rs` documents; the canonical check is `cargo test -p richview-core --test palettes`.
