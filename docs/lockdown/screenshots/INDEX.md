# Screenshot index

Screenshots and text captures for the RichView lockdown, taken on 21 and 22 October 2026. `product/` shows main at `b4db233` from the local release build; `styles-design/`, `report-rendering/`, `surfaces/` and `delivery/` hold renders that other lockdown stages made from the same commit. `branches/` shows features of the open PRs #62 to #65, built from their heads or rendered with main's build. `live/` and `open-work/` show the public sites, which do not match main: richview.ai served PR #69 content and news.richview.ai served PR #70. Sizes: in the repository, this folder holds 216 images and 20 text files, this index included (22.8 MB), and `docs/guide/img/` holds 47 images (3.9 MB): 26.7 MB in total. The 106 local-only images take 17.5 MB. 1 MB = 1,000,000 bytes.

## Right-sizing (22 October 2026)

The repository keeps every image that a part file, a guide page, a lockdown file or an evidence file references. The 103 captures that only this index listed moved to `~/tmp/richview-lockdown-20261021/screenshots-local/` on Claw Mini, under the same relative paths, together with `styles-design/showcase-sleek-1440.png`, a byte-identical copy of `live/richview-flagship-sleek-desktop-viewport.png`; the references to that copy now point to the live file. The rows of moved files keep their capture details and read "local only (not committed)"; their names are not links, because the files are not in the repository. The two PR #64 captures, `branches/pr64-dashboard-rendered.jpg` and `branches/pr64-cross-selection.jpg`, moved there later on 22 October for another reason: they show business metrics from a demo dataset whose provenance is unconfirmed, so no card or guide page embeds them.

The committed images were then recompressed in place, keeping every file name:

- Every JPEG and PNG was losslessly re-optimised (mozjpeg for JPEG, oxipng for PNG); decoded pixels are unchanged.
- JPEGs saved above quality 75 were re-encoded at quality 72, 4:2:0, progressive, at the same pixel size: the 21 `live/` section viewports (quality 85), the six `surfaces/` captures (80 to 82), one `open-work/` crop (80) and 37 guide images (76 to 95).
- The twelve `product/` desktop full pages were resampled to 75% (1,080 px wide, 1,094 px for the landing page) at quality 72, as the `live/` desktop full pages already were; their rows give both pixel sizes.
- `product/showcase/showcase-scientific-desktop-full-part3of3.jpg`, the one other file over 400 KB, was re-encoded at quality 72. `live/richview-docs-publishing-desktop-full.jpg` stays at 444 KB at its quality 70.

Nothing was upscaled. The pre-change copies of the committed files stay in the gitignored `tmp/rightsize/backup/` of this worktree.

## How product/ was captured

Build: `target/release/richview` and `target/release/richview-server` (3.0.0-alpha.0) and the staged site `dist/web`, all from `b4db233` with no local source changes. Site pages came from `richview-server` on a random loopback port with a temporary data directory and `--public-dir dist/web`. Fixture reports came from a loopback static server over their `Rendered/` folder. Nothing was published or deployed, and each run stopped its servers.

Browser: headless Chromium 153.0.8010.12 from the repository's Playwright 1.63.0, with a fresh temporary user-data directory per device profile, light colour scheme, `en-US` and Europe/Prague time. Desktop is 1440×900 at device scale 1. Phone is 390×844 with touch emulation, at device scale 2 for viewport and element captures and at device scale 1 for full pages, so phone full pages are 390 px wide.

Settling: each capture waited for `document.fonts.ready`, for `window.__richview.settled()` where the page has the runtime, for two animation frames and then 0.8 to 2 more seconds (fixture full pages also waited for the YouTube frame and 4 seconds); Playwright finished any running CSS transitions at capture time. Full pages were scrolled end to end first so lazy charts and iframes mounted. Images were saved as JPEG quality 75, 4:2:0, progressive; the committed copies were right-sized on 22 October (see Right-sizing). Full pages taller than about 9,200 px are split into equal parts named `-partNofM`; for showcase full pages the limit is 6,900 px on desktop after 50% scaling and 13,800 px on the phone.

Paths in the tables are relative to this folder. URLs are paths on the loopback server. Capture scripts, per-run manifests and the rendered fixture HTML stay in the gitignored `tmp/shots/` of this worktree; the raw PNGs were deleted after conversion.
