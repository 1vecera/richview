# Original illustrative photos

These photographs illustrated the 2 October 2026 report. They are retained for snapshot history; the active report now uses the [15 October editorial image revision](../assets/CREDITS.md). They are not evidence: no finding, number or claim depends on them, and they are not part of the dated source ledger. Each was cropped to a wide banner, resized to 1600 px and converted to WebP for this report.
