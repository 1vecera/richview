# Capability race: findings, contradictions and gaps

Scope: `capability.csv` (115 rows, K001 to K115; 80 independently measured, 35 lab-reported) and `releases.csv` (73 rows, M001 to M073). Row IDs are cited in brackets. Dataset provenance is in `capability_datasets.md`. As of 2026-10-02, no lab leads across the board, and the margins at the top are mostly inside measurement noise.

## Findings

- Through 2023, OpenAI's GPT-4 had no peer. It held #1 on Chatbot Arena from its addition in May 2023 until Claude 3 Opus tied it in March 2024 [K004]; the AI Index 2026 puts OpenAI at 1,322 Arena points against Google's 1,117 in early 2023 [K069 notes]. It was also the 2023 frontier on Epoch's capability index (ECI) and on METR's time horizon [K001, K002].
- On composite capability, OpenAI usually held the lead from the first reasoning model in September 2024 until mid-2026. It kept the ECI frontier from o1-preview through GPT-5.5 Pro, except while Gemini 2.5 Pro and Gemini 3 Pro were on top [K015, K076]. On the Artificial Analysis (AA) Intelligence Index it lost the lead to xAI's Grok 4 in mid-2025, regained it with GPT-5, ceded it to Gemini 3 Pro, ended up in a three-way tie in April 2026, and retook it with GPT-5.5 [K031, K033, K043, K070, K075].
- Anthropic's composite lead began in June 2026. Claude Fable 5 opened about 5 points ahead on the AA index [K083], and by late September Claude Opus 5.5 topped both AA (58) and the ECI (167.35) [K104, K105]. Those leads fall within the confidence intervals: GPT-6 Astra tied Fable 5.1 on AA [K102], and its ECI interval overlaps Opus 5.5's [K094, K105].
- In agentic software engineering, Anthropic has the longest run. Its self-reported SWE-bench Verified score was the best for most of October 2024 to April 2026 [K011, K020, K028, K032, K040, K048]. OpenAI was ahead only briefly or on non-matching setups: o3 at 69.1%, and GPT-5 at 74.9% on a 477-task subset against Claude Opus 4.1's 74.5% on all 500 [K026, K037]; GPT-5.2's 80.0% trailed Claude Opus 4.5's 80.9% [K054]. Epoch's highest independent run is Claude Opus 4.7 at 0.835, against 0.806 for OpenAI's best [K072].
- Terminal-Bench 2.0 is the clear exception, and OpenAI leads it: GPT-5.3-Codex at 77.3% and GPT-5.5 at 82.7%, against 65 to 69% for Claude Opus 4.6 and 4.7 [K060, K061, K079]. The top independent leaderboard entry is GPT-5.5 inside a third-party agent harness (0.847) [K080]. Only Anthropic's gated Mythos Preview beats it, and only under relaxed timeouts [K068].
- Anthropic opened the computer-use category in October 2024 [K010]. OpenAI's CUA led OSWorld from January to May 2025 (38.1%) [K017]. From Claude Sonnet 4 in May 2025 through February 2026 Anthropic led again [K039, K059], before OpenAI reached parity: GPT-5.4 at 75.0% against a 72.4% human baseline, and GPT-5.5 at 78.7% against Claude Opus 4.7's 78.0% [K065, K078]. On OSWorld 2.0, Claude Opus 5 leads the only independent file, which has no GPT-6 Astra entry [K089].
- In math, OpenAI dominates. Nearly every record on Epoch's FrontierMath series belongs to OpenAI [K051, K077, K096, K111]. Anthropic set a record once, Claude Fable 5 on Tier 4 [K085], and Epoch finds Claude models underperform on math relative to their overall ECI [K081].
- On METR's long-task time horizon, the record swapped between the two labs on almost every release from mid-2024 [K008, K013, K019, K025, K036, K047, K053]. Anthropic has held it since Claude Opus 4.6 in February 2026 (719 minutes), and Claude Mythos Preview (early) sits at about 1,045 minutes [K058, K066]. OpenAI's best published figure is 352 minutes, for GPT-5.2 [K053].
- In human-preference chat (the LMArena text leaderboard), OpenAI was #1 for most of the period to spring 2025 [K006, K009, K027]. The exceptions were Gemini-Exp-1114 tying in November 2024 [K012], Gemini 2.5 Pro Experimental debuting at #1 in March 2025 [K022], and brief DeepSeek-R1 and Grok 3 leads that appear in the dataset. Google then held #1 from June 2025 to February 2026, interrupted briefly by GPT-5 and Claude Sonnet 4.5 [K030, K035, K041, K044]. Anthropic was #1 from 2026-02-06 to 2026-09-29 [K062, K087, K110]. Google's Gemini 4 Argon took #1 on 2026-09-30 while available only to trusted testers [K112].
- Anthropic has led LMArena's WebDev/Code Arena since November 2025, with short interruptions by GPT-5.6 Sol, Kimi K3 and GPT-6 Astra (2026-09-05 to 2026-09-22) [K086, K101, K114].
- In long-horizon business agents, Anthropic leads Artificial Analysis's GDPval-AA (Claude Opus 4.7 at 1,753 Elo; Claude Opus 5.5 at 1,846 against GPT-6 Astra's 1,542) [K071, K106]. On OpenAI's own GDPval, GPT-5.2 claims 70.9% wins or ties against professionals [K052]. Andon Labs' Vending-Bench 2 had Anthropic models at #1 from February 2026 until GPT-6 Astra took over with $15,515 [K090, K115].
- ARC-AGI shows the race most clearly. OpenAI defined ARC-AGI-1 in 2024 [K014, K016]. ARC-AGI-2's record then passed between labs: Claude Opus 4.6, then Gemini 3 Deep Think, GPT-5.5, Claude Fable 5, GPT-5.6 Sol and GPT-6 Astra (95%) [K056, K074, K082, K092]. On ARC-AGI-3, OpenAI is far ahead [K093].
- The independent assessments agree on near-parity. The State of AI Report 2025 found "OpenAI retains a narrow lead" [K042]. The AI Index 2025 measured the top-two Arena gap at 0.7% [K023]. The AI Index 2026 places four labs within 25 Arena points, with Anthropic first [K069]. METR calls Claude Opus 5.5 an "on-trend" step over Fable 5.1 [K107].

## Contradictions

- SWE-bench Verified scores are not like-for-like. OpenAI ran a 477-task subset while Anthropic used all 500 tasks [K028 notes, K037], and Epoch measured GPT-5 at 0.7355 against OpenAI's own 74.9% [K037, K038]. OpenAI later declared the benchmark contaminated [K063], and in July 2026 it withdrew its recommendation of SWE-Bench Pro as well [K063 notes].
- On 2026-02-05, both labs claimed the top Terminal-Bench 2.0 score using different harnesses [K060, K061]. On Terminal-Bench 4.0, Anthropic reports Claude Opus 5.5 at 66.4% against GPT-6 Astra's 57.9%, while AA measured the two at parity (59.6%) [K104, K108].
- ARC-AGI-3: OpenAI says GPT-6 Astra "saturates" it at 99.9%, but ARC Prize's standard harness gives 62.7%; the 99.9% needs the provider adapter harness [K093, K095].
- On the ECI, Epoch's hub page names GPT-6 Astra the top model, while the downloaded CSV ranks Claude Opus 5.5 first [K094, K105]. The record shifts between refits too: Epoch's September 1 insight put Claude Fable 5 above GPT-5.5 Pro, and the October file puts it below [K076, K084, K091 notes].
- Humanity's Last Exam has no settled leader. Epoch's external file has GPT-6 Astra at 54.8%, ahead of Anthropic [K099]. AA has Claude Opus 5.5 at 61.4% [K104]. Anthropic's own table reports 67.7% "with tools" [K099 notes]. OpenAI's 2025 deep-research figure (26.6%) used browsing [K018].
- LMArena figures disagree. GPT-4o's pre-release alias scored 1309 while the public entry scored 1289 [K005, K006]. The official history dataset omits the experimental Gemini models that Google and the press reported at #1 [K012, K022].
- The AA index changed version three times in 2026, so the top scores 64.9, 60, 53 and 58 are not comparable [K083, K088, K102, K104].
- Capability benchmarks and human preference diverge. GPT-6 Astra leads ARC-AGI-2, FrontierMath, GPQA, Humanity's Last Exam and Vending-Bench 2, yet ranks 30th on the LMArena text leaderboard [K092, K098, K099, K113, K115]. Newer is not always higher, either: Claude Opus 5.5 ranks below Claude Opus 5 on Vending-Bench 2 [K115].

## Gaps

- METR has published no time horizon since April 2026, so GPT-5.5, Claude Opus 4.7 and 4.8, Fable 5, GPT-6 Astra and Opus 5.5 are unmeasured; the Opus 5.5 report is qualitative [K066 notes, K107].
- Gated and paused models muddy "who leads". Claude Mythos Preview, Mythos 5 and Mythos 5.1, Gemini 4 Argon and the phased GPT-6 Astra rollout were not generally available, and Claude Fable 5 was suspended under US export controls from 2026-06-12 to 2026-07-01 [M061, M063, K088].
- About 30% of rows (35) are lab-reported and not independently reproduced. Several publication dates are approximate: the AA Q2 2025 report [K031], the date of OpenAI's SWE-bench post (taken from aggregators) [K063], and a live leaderboard recorded on the day it was viewed [K115].
- AA index history before 2025 and its exact dates are not captured. Comparable independent coding evaluations after SWE-bench Verified (FrontierCode, DeepSWE, Terminal-Bench 4.0) have only a few months of history.
- The release list omits GPT-4V, GPT-5.3 Instant and GPT-5.5 Cyber, and its Google and xAI coverage is context only (no Gemini 3.6 to 3.8 Flash or Grok 4.x rows).
