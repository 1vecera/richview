# Commercial position: OpenAI vs Anthropic — discovery notes

Ledger: `commercial.csv` (207 rows, C001–C207; 56 primary, 100 press, 28 survey, 23 analyst). Retrieved 2026-10-02 via Exa. Quotes are verbatim, except that apostrophes and quotation marks are normalised, zero-width characters and footnote markers removed, and line breaks in LinkedIn lists collapsed. Neither company has a public S-1 on EDGAR, so every 2024–2026 income-statement figure below comes from leaked or confidential documents reported by Reuters, the FT, the WSJ, Bloomberg or Ed Zitron. Run-rates come from the companies or from unnamed sources, and none of them is audited.

## Findings

- **Run-rate lead flipped, then the gap widened.** Anthropic's stated run-rate went from about $1bn (Jan 2025, C082) to more than $5bn (Aug 2025, C085), about $9bn (end-2025, C092), $14bn (Feb 2026, C098), more than $30bn (Apr 2026, C105), more than $47bn (May, C107) and more than $65bn (end-July, C112/C113). The NYT reports it at or above $100bn in September (C114/C115). OpenAI went from about $5.5bn (Dec 2024, C001) to $21.4bn (end-2025, C010), more than $25bn (Feb 2026), $2bn a month (Mar, C011), more than $40bn (Jul/Aug, C017) and close to $70bn (late Sept, C021/C023). Anthropic first appeared ahead in April 2026 (C159).
- **Recognised revenue tells a slower story.** For FY2025, OpenAI booked $13.07bn (C009) against Anthropic's roughly $4.6bn (C093). OpenAI still led in Q1 2026 ($5.7bn against $4.73–4.8bn; C015, C110, C160). Anthropic overtook it in Q2 2026, with more than $11.5bn (preliminary) against $6.7bn (C110, C015).
- **Profitability diverged in 2026.** OpenAI's operating loss was $20.92bn in FY2025 (C065) and $12.3bn in Q2 2026, including stock-based compensation (C074). Anthropic's FY2025 operating loss was more than $8bn (C144). Its roughly $42bn net loss includes a non-cash charge of about $34bn (C145). Anthropic reported positive *adjusted* operating income for Q2 2026 and expects the same for Q3 (C110, C157), but has published neither a figure nor the definition.
- **Forward burn and projections.** An OpenAI deck seen by the FT projects $278bn of negative free cash flow over 2026–2030 (C078), with revenue rising from $36bn in 2026 to $350bn in 2030 (C026). Anthropic reportedly projects $190–200bn of revenue for 2028 (C119), against its own late-2025 plan of $70bn (C118). Its investors expect the run-rate to reach $100–120bn by the end of 2026 (C117).
- **Valuation ladder.** OpenAI was valued at $157bn (C028), then $300bn (C029), $500bn in a secondary sale (C030) and $852bn post-money (C033/C034). It is now in talks at about $1.4tn pre-money (C042). Anthropic went from $61.5bn (C120) to $183bn (C122), $380bn (C124) and $965bn (C128), and is reportedly targeting about $2tn in an IPO raising up to $100bn in November (C131–C133). OpenAI has ruled out a 2026 IPO (C041).
- **Mix favours Anthropic on API and enterprise.** In FY2025, about $3.8bn of Anthropic's revenue was usage-based and $0.79bn came from subscriptions (C095). Enterprise customers make up about 80% (C088). At OpenAI, about 70% of revenue came from consumers in October 2025 (C005), with enterprise above 40% by March 2026 (C013). OpenAI's B2B revenue has grown more than 100% since July 2026 (C022).
- **Coding is the main growth engine.** Claude Code's run-rate was $0.5bn in September 2025 (C086), $1bn in November (C089) and more than $2.5bn in February 2026 (C099). Anthropic has disclosed nothing since. Codex weekly users grew from 1.6m to more than 5m (C051, C056). OpenAI's combined Codex and ChatGPT Work product had 20m weekly users in August and 35m in September (C020, C059). Only model-based estimates put a value on Codex (C164).
- **Consumer reach is still overwhelmingly OpenAI's.** ChatGPT weekly users grew from 500m (C043) to 800m (C046), 900m (C050) and 1.2bn (C058). Sensor Tower puts ChatGPT's app at 1bn monthly users against Claude's 56m (C186). Similarweb's August 2026 web share is ChatGPT 55.5%, Gemini 25.6% and Claude 9.3% (C195). Neither company publishes Claude user counts.
- **Business adoption on Ramp crossed over.** Ramp measures the share of US businesses paying each vendor. Anthropic went from 11.1% (Jul 2025) to 43.8% (Aug 2026), while OpenAI went from 35.1% to 39.8% (C168, C196). The crossover came in April 2026 (C185). Customer bases overlap heavily (C179).
- **Enterprise share depends on the survey.** Menlo, an Anthropic investor, puts Anthropic at 40% and OpenAI at 27% of enterprise API spend at the end of 2025 (C174), with Anthropic at 54% in coding (C175). a16z, an OpenAI investor, gives OpenAI about 56% of Global-2000 wallet share (C176).
- **Developer gateways show another picture.** In the week of 21 September 2026, OpenRouter's request share was DeepSeek 24.4%, Google 20.5%, OpenAI 17.9% and Anthropic 2.7% (C201). Chinese open-weight models dominate token volume (C197).
- **Concentration and channel risk sit with Anthropic.** Two unnamed customers each brought in 12% of 2025 revenue (C094). Press reports name them as Cursor and GitHub Copilot (C084), and Cursor is now owned by SpaceX (C203). Cloud marketplaces carried 47% of sales (C096). At both companies, the top 1% of customers bring in about 80% of revenue (C200).
- **Both companies hold very large compute commitments.** Anthropic has $518bn of obligations (C156) and had $54.6bn of non-cancellable commitments at the end of 2025 (C148). Its main deals are AWS (more than $100bn over 10 years, C150), Azure ($30bn, C142) and SpaceX ($1.25bn a month, C152). OpenAI cited $1.4tn for 30 GW (C061), then set a target of about $600bn by 2030 (C068). The FT reports about $856bn of compute spend through 2030 (C079).
- **Business counts are defined differently.** OpenAI counts 1m paying business customers (Nov 2025, C047), 2m businesses reached (Aug 2026, C057) and 2.5m (Sept, C059). Anthropic reported more than 300k business customers in September 2025 (C134) and more than 1,000 customers spending over $1m (Apr 2026, C138).

## Contradictions

- **Anthropic FY2025 revenue.** CNBC repeatedly writes "roughly $10bn" (C091). The prospectus figure reported by Reuters is about $4.6bn (C093), and The Information gives 2024 as $381m (C081), against $386m in secondary relays of the prospectus.
- **Anthropic Q2 2026.** The WSJ projection in May was $10.9bn with $559m of operating profit (C109, C155). The preliminary result is more than $11.5bn with an unquantified adjusted profit (C110, C111). One outlet reports $11.6bn (C163). The $559m is a projection, not a result.
- **OpenAI valuation.** Axios's "$730bn" (C037) is the February pre-money figure (C032), not a separate mark from the $852bn post-money valuation.
- **OpenAI ARR at the end of 2024.** Reuters and CNBC report $5.5bn (C001), while the CFO's blog gives $6bn (C008).
- **Gross versus net revenue.** OpenAI says Anthropic's $30bn run-rate is overstated by about $8bn (C161). The prospectus shows only about $351m of 2025 distribution fees (C096 notes), although that figure is at 2025 scale.
- **Margins use incompatible definitions.** Anthropic's gross margin appears as 40% for 2025 (C143), about 44% for 2026 according to PitchBook (C154), and above 80% before revenue-sharing and training costs (C157). OpenAI's 2025 margin appears as 46% projected (C064) and 33% according to Sacra (C075).
- **Two disputed run-rate data points.** The March 2026 $19bn figure was attributed to the CEO (C101), but a transcript says the Morgan Stanley host said it (C102). The September $100bn appears both as "now pacing" (C115) and as "expected this year" (C114).
- **Breaks inside the share series.** Ramp changed its methodology in June 2026 (C187), and its "invoice share" (more than 60%, C180) differs from its adoption share (24.4%, C181). Similarweb revises earlier data points (C192 against C195). OpenRouter's token shares (C189, C197) and request share (C201) also diverge.
- **OpenAI's compute plan keeps moving.** It went from $1.4tn (C061) to $600bn (C068) to $856bn (C079). Axios and Reuters also word the Q3 consumer-revenue figure differently (C024, C025).

## Gaps not filled

- **No audited S-1 for either company.** Anthropic's gross margin, its Q3 2026 results and the identity of its top customers remain unconfirmed.
- **No Anthropic user or subscriber counts.** Anthropic has published no MAU or WAU and no paid-subscriber count; it has said only that paid subscriptions "more than doubled" (C137). Its business-customer total has not been updated since September 2025.
- **No 2026 product revenue for either side.** Anthropic has given no Claude Code revenue since February 2026. OpenAI has given no official Codex revenue, no consumer-versus-enterprise split beyond "more than 40%", and no subscriber count after 50m.
- **No 2026 Menlo update.** No Menlo Ventures report for 2026 was found, and no a16z figure for Anthropic's wallet share was given.
- **OpenAI's latest quarter is missing.** No OpenAI Q3 2026 recognised revenue was found, and the composition of the Q2 operating loss is not broken down.
- **Unconfirmed secondary sale.** A roughly $7bn OpenAI secondary at $852bn appears only in Sacra and TFN (C039).
