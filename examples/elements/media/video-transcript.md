Silent archival excerpt: a television view of Buzz Aldrin on the Apollo 11 lunar module ladder. He holds the ladder with one foot stretched down, then drops to the footpad and stands on it. The picture is soft and high in contrast. This 12-second excerpt begins 41 seconds into NASA's two-minute moonwalk montage (Apollo_11_moonwalk_montage_720p), made from the television video NASA partially restored in 2009; its soundtrack is omitted.
