Life expectancy at birth rose in every region between 1950 and 2019

Gain in years, by region. Population-weighted regional averages; differences rounded to one decimal.

Interpretation

Life expectancy at birth was higher in 2019 than in 1950 in all four regions. Asia gained the most (29.3 years, from 45.6 to 74.9) and Europe the least (14.2 years, from 64.6 to 78.8). Africa gained 22.4 years and the Americas 16.7 years.

Regions that started lower tended to gain more. The gap between the highest and lowest regional values narrowed from 21.8 years in 1950 (Europe vs. Africa) to 13.6 years in 2019 (Europe vs. Africa). Africa still had the lowest value in 2019, at 65.2 years.
