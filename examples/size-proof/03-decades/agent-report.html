What the data show

Asia has the only decline, −4.9 years in 1950–1960, followed by the largest gain, +14.5 years in 1960–1970. Africa’s gain is +0.7 years in 1990–2000 and +5.4 years in 2000–2010. The Americas and Europe gain life expectancy in every supplied period.
