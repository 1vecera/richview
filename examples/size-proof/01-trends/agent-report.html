Regional trends

Interpretation

Life expectancy is higher in 2019 than in 1950 in all four regions. In 2019, Europe records 78.8 years, followed by the Americas (77.5), Asia (74.9) and Africa (65.2). Asia shows a sharp dip to 40.7 years in 1960, while Africa’s series is comparatively flat during the 1990s.
