Life expectancy at birth: Europe minus Africa, 1950–2019

Population-weighted regional averages, in years, rounded to one decimal. The gap is Europe minus Africa.

Gap by year

Interpretation

The gap between Europe and Africa was 21.8 years in 1950 and rose to its maximum of 23.0 years in 1960 and 1961. It then narrowed to 16.6 years by 1994–1995, widened again to 18.1 years in 2001, and has fallen almost every year since, reaching its minimum of 13.6 years in 2019. Over the whole period it narrowed by 8.2 years (21.8 to 13.6).

Both averages are higher in 2019 than in 1950: Europe rose from 64.6 to 78.8 years (+14.2) and Africa from 42.8 to 65.2 years (+22.4). Africa's larger increase accounts for the narrowing. The gap compares two regional averages and says nothing about differences within either region.
