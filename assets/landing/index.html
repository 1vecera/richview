RichView is a local CLI that lets you generate beautiful HTML reports from simple Git-storable text files.

Try it in your browser ↓

Agents create HTML reports by default. They're large, hard to edit, hard to version and iterate on, and different every time.

There's a better way: RichView.

How does RichView work?

RichView is a CLI that works entirely offline to turn a text specification into a beautiful, interactive HTML file with charts, images and more.

The broader ecosystem adds publishing, a VS Code extension, slides, brand styles and backgrounds.

  1. Write a specification

    Describe your text, charts and images in a file.

    richview init my-report
    ↳ my-report/report.rmd

  2. Convert it to HTML

    Turn your local files into one self-contained HTML file.

    richview generate my-report/report.rmd

  3. Send it to a colleague

    They can open the file in their browser.

    my-report/Rendered/report.html

  4. Publish it for a link

    Sign in to a RichView server and choose who can open the link.

    richview publish my-report/report.rmd

Everything runs on your computer: no account, no upload, no AI calls of its own. Publishing is optional.

    The best elements of Rich Markdown

    • Charts

      RichView has its own charting library: chart types, from line and bar to Sankey diagrams, treemaps and maps, each drawn from the data beside it. The chart gallery shows them, with flow charts and pivot tables on pages of their own.

      Life expectancy at birth, 1950–2019. Data: Gapminder (CC BY 4.0).

    • Metrics

      The one number that matters, with what it counts and how it moved.

      78.8Years a newborn in Europe could expect to live, 2019+14.2 years since 1950

    • Callouts

      An aside that tells the reader which way the evidence points.

      Why the dip. The sharp fall in Asia around 1960 is the Great Chinese Famine of 1959–61.

    • Tables

      Exact values in columns a reader can sort.

      Years gained, 1950 to 2019
      Region19502019Gained
      Asia45.674.929.3
      Africa42.865.222.4
      Americas60.877.516.7
      Europe64.678.814.2

    • Images

      Your own pictures, or credited photos from Unsplash.

    • Typography

      Curly quotes, dashes and footnotes, set as a typesetter would.

      “Longer lives” – 1950 to 20191

    • Colour

      Each style brings its own accent; RichView derives the tints from it and checks every palette for contrast and colour vision.
