`slides: true` in the frontmatter turns the source into slides. Each top-level heading or `---` starts a slide. The style's stage sets the look. Slides are for a screen: big numbers, charts and short text, one screen each. Remove the line and the same source is a report again.

- **A slide starts** at each top-level heading of the body (`#` when the body uses it, otherwise `##`), which is its title, and at each `---` line, which starts a slide without a title. The frontmatter title, description, author and date are the title slide. A heading with nothing under it is a section slide.
- **Write what is on a slide, never where it sits.** A slide shows its title, then its `metric` blocks as a row of big numbers, then its charts, tables and images, then its text. One `metric` alone on a slide is the hero number. One figure takes the room left, two stand side by side, and text beside a single figure is a narrow column; a table alone keeps the width, with its text under it, and is set large.
- **A big number is a `metric`**: `value`, `label`, the change as `delta` (a fraction) and what it is compared with as `delta_label`, such as `vs October`. A value written as text, such as `"$630K"`, is shown as written, while `format: number_compact` spells the scale out (`$630 thousand`). A chart on a slide prints its values as in a report and hides its actions; put the numbers that matter in metrics too.
- **Keep a slide to one screen.** `slides.density` warns when a slide holds more than four numbers beside a figure (eight alone), more than two figures, more than 50 words beside a figure or number (80 alone) or a table of more than ten rows. Start a new slide instead: a heading or a `---` line.
- **Titles state the claim**, as chart titles do, and the source note stays under its chart. `tabs` has no effect in slides (`slides.tabs`), and neither do `collapsed_sections` or `::: columns`: a slide shows everything it holds, in its own places.
- **The style is the stage.** Each builtin sets its slides in a world of its own (Sleek a stage at night, Air daylight, Brutalist posters, Hand-drawn a drawn talk, Scientific a paper's talk), and slides below the first screen build as they arrive. A style file chooses with `components.slides.stage`; nothing in the source changes.
- **Write for the stage.** A slide whose only figure is one chart shows the chart's `title` as its headline: write it as the takeaway, and the heading as the topic. Put the headline metric first in a row; it leads. In a Markdown table a column of signed changes (`+50%`) shows as chips and a last row equal to the sum of those above as the total. Put the series the slide is about first; the others step back.
- **Readers** move with Space, Page Down and the arrows; `F` fills the screen and `#slide=4` opens the fourth slide. Printing and `richview generate --to pdf` give one 16:9 page per slide. A published story does not frame slides yet: publish the source without `slides: true`.
