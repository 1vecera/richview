    How answering works

    Accept
    Keep the proposal as written. On a card with options, take the recommended option.
    Edit
    Write your own wording. It saves as you type and replaces the proposal; no Accept needed afterwards.
    Choose
    On a card with options, pick one. That is your whole answer.
    Reject
    Drop it. The rewrite does not build or follow it.
    Defer
    Decide later. It stays open.
    Note
    A comment for the agents, not an answer. On an undecided card it is saved with your next decision.
    Clear answer
    Remove your decision.

    Every answer saves at once; the card then says what was saved. ? opens and closes this, Esc closes it.
